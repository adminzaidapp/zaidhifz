Tajweed Guide

The colour coding follows the standard Hafs Tajweed Quran.

Ikhfa — Light Blue
Hiding the noon sakinah or tanween with a nasal sound when followed by one of 15 letters.
مِن شَرِّ
Ghunnah — Orange
A nasal humming sound held for two counts on a shaddah over noon or meem.
إِنَّ — النَّاسِ
Ikhfa Meem Saakin — Pink
When meem saakin is followed by ba, the meem is hidden with a nasal sound.
هُم بِهِ
Idgham — Dark Green
Merging the noon sakinah or tanween into the following letter.
مِن نَّعِيمٍ
Qalqalah — Red
A slight echoing sound on the letters ق ط ب ج د when they appear with sukoon.
قُلْ — الْفَلَقِ
Qalb — Purple
When noon sakinah or tanween is followed by ba, the noon converts to a meem sound.
مِنۢ بَعْدِ
Idgham Meem Saakin — Light Green
When meem saakin is followed by another meem, they merge with a ghunnah of two counts.
لَهُم مَّا
