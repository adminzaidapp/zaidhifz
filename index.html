Tajweed Guide

The colour coding follows the standard Hafs Tajweed Quran.

Ikhfa — Light Blue
Hiding the noon sakinah or tanween with a nasal sound.
مِن شَرِّ
Ghunnah — Orange
A nasal humming sound held for two counts on a shaddah over noon or meem.
إِنَّ — النَّاسِ
Ikhfa Meem Saakin — Pink
When meem saakin is followed by ba, hidden with a nasal sound.
هُم بِهِ
Idgham — Dark Green
Merging the noon sakinah or tanween into the following letter.
مِن نَّعِيمٍ
Qalqalah — Red
A slight echoing sound on the letters ق ط ب ج د with sukoon.
قُلْ — الْفَلَقِ
Qalb — Purple
Noon sakinah followed by ba converts to a meem sound.
مِنۢ بَعْدِ
Idgham Meem Saakin — Light Green
Meem saakin followed by meem merge with ghunnah.
لَهُم مَّا
